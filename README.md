Build and deploy a standalone, high-converting French-language website for local master electrician business "Frank Mastro l'Électricien" (Montréal & Rive-Sud).

Strict requirements:
1. Language: 100% French throughout the entire site (navigation, buttons, forms, modals, SEO meta, badges). Zero English.
2. 2-Second Electric Logo Reveal: An electrifying 2-second preloader/intro animation with lightning/electric voltage sparks revealing the attached logo before cleanly transitioning to the main site.
3. Hero Section:
   - Features the attached bespoke cinematic video (hero_video_1.mp4) playing seamlessly, muted, looping, with poster fallback.
   - Headline: "SERVICES ÉLECTRIQUES RAPIDES ET EFFICACES à Montréal et en Rive-Sud".

   - Emergency call badge & tap-to-call: 514-497-2096 (Urgences 24/7) and 514-497-2126.

   - Warning points: Fils vieux et défectueux, Mauvais usage de fils d'extension, Usage excessif d'électricité, Court-circuit et entretien médiocre.
